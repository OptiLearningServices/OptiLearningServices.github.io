About me

Mathematics as a tool for understanding and optimizing complex systems.

I am a mathematician and researcher working at the intersection of dynamical systems, optimization, optimal control and mathematical modelling.

My research focuses on the development of mathematical models and computational methods capable of describing, analyzing and controlling complex systems.

I am particularly interested in applications where mathematical modelling can contribute to more efficient, reliable and sustainable systems, including energy, environmental and resource-management applications.

My approach combines mathematical analysis, numerical optimization, simulation and data-driven methods to transform complex scientific questions into computationally tractable problems.

def grad_f(x, func):
    value = np.zeros(len(x))
    xd = x.copy()
    h = 1e-6

    for j in range(len(x)):
        xd[j] = x[j] + h
        value[j] = (func(xd) - func(x)) / h
        xd = x.copy()
    return value
